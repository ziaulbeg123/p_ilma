## What it is
A personal e-portfolio website for Ilma Muskan, a CTET-qualified educator. It rebuilds the reference site (shreya-build…preview.emergentagent.com) with the same person, content and photos.

## Who it's for
- School principals, recruiters and hiring panels who are reviewing her for teaching roles
- Anyone checking her qualifications, certificates and event participation

- **Contact & Verification:** Bareilly, Uttar Pradesh; Invertis University; ilmaparvez85@gmail.com (opens an email); 7017404916 (tap to call).
